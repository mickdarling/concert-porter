Get your concert list

Two ways to do this. The first is quicker — no email, no waiting.

Fetch them straight from Concert Archives — recommended
  1. 1
    What's your Concert Archives username? It's the name in your profile's web address — concertarchives.org/yourname.
  2. 2
    Make sure your bookmarks bar is showing, then drag this button onto it: Mac: press ⌘⇧B  ·  Windows: Ctrl+Shift+B 🎤 Grab my shows
  3. 3
    Open your concert page, then click that new bookmark. It reads every page of your history and brings you straight back here — nothing to copy. Open my Concert Archives page ↗
Type your username above and these two buttons will wake up.
Or use the emailed spreadsheet — works everywhere, takes a few minutes longer
  1. 1
    Open concertarchives.org and sign in.Same account you've always used.
  2. 2
    Click your name (top right), then “Export To Spreadsheet.”One click — that's the whole export.
  3. 3
    Check your email and download the file.It usually arrives within a couple of minutes. It's a “.csv” file.

On an iPhone or iPad — two ways

Easiest: do the computer steps above on any computer, then sign in on whativeheard.com there. Your shows sync to this device on their own — nothing else to do here.

Or right here in Safari:

  1. 1
    Tap this button:
  2. 2
    Open whativeheard.com and bookmark it.Share button → “Add Bookmark” → Save.
  3. 3
    Swap the bookmark's address for the code.Bookmarks (book icon) → Edit → tap the new bookmark → clear the address, paste, Save.

It only adds — anything you'd already ticked stays put, and you can untick any show on the site if something's wrong. Sign in on What I've Heard afterwards and your history follows you to your phone and other computers.

The archive only knows shows that fans have logged on setlist.fm. These nights aren't there yet, so they can't come across. You can download the list — and if you add those shows on setlist.fm, they'll appear in What I've Heard for everyone.
