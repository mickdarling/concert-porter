CONCERT PORTER →

Port your Concert Archives history into What I've Heard — matched through the setlist.fm archive, injected without a single click-through.

1Your Concert Archives export

On concertarchives.org, open your user menu → Export To Spreadsheet. The CSV arrives by email. Drop it here.

2setlist.fm access

The setlist.fm API doesn't allow direct browser calls, so requests go through a small relay. Use the default relay, or your own (see the repo's worker/ folder). An API key is free — if the relay has its own key you can leave yours blank.

3Match your shows

Each show is looked up on setlist.fm by artist + date, then scored against your venue and city. Lookups are throttled to respect setlist.fm's rate limits and cached locally, so you can stop and resume.

An independent helper tool. Not affiliated with Concert Archives, What I've Heard, or setlist.fm. Concert data is read live from the setlist.fm API under their terms; nothing you upload leaves your browser except the artist + date lookups.
